Why B2BStays

What Makes Us Different

Most hotel software was built for direct bookings and then stretched to cover B2B. The gaps show up every month-end.

Built for B2B, not adapted from B2C.

Every module, from agent bookings to city ledger, was built around how hotel B2B actually runs. Not patched onto a generic PMS.

Agent-facing and hotel-facing. Both.

Agents submit requests through their own portal. The hotel team reviews, confirms and tracks without back-and-forth over WhatsApp or email.

Commission tracked at booking, not month-end.

Every confirmed booking logs its commission instantly. No reconciliation runs. No disputes. One click settles via NEFT.

Vendors escalate themselves. No chasing.

Service requests go to your vendor list automatically. No reply in the window and the next vendor is contacted, staff alerted. Zero manual follow-up.

All modules share the same data.

A confirmed booking updates the ledger, triggers commission tracking and notifies fleet automatically. No re-entry, no sync delays, no data silos.
