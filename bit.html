The essentials
Three questions every hotel GM asks.
Here are the honest answers.
hotel
What is it?
One platform that runs every B2B workflow your hotel already has — just without the spreadsheets.
B2BStays connects agent bookings, city ledger, commission payable, fleet, WhatsApp campaigns and vendor coordination into a single system built specifically for hotel operations. Not a generic CRM adapted for hospitality.
warning_amber
Why do I need it?
Most hotels run B2B on a mix of WhatsApp threads, Excel files and memory. That works — until it doesn't.
When a commission dispute lands with no audit trail, a vendor misses a pickup because no one followed up, or a city ledger goes unreconciled for weeks, the cost is real. B2BStays is what fills the gap your PMS was never designed for.
trending_up
How will it help me?
Your team stops chasing confirmations. Your accounts team stops reconciling manually. Your agents stop calling to check status.
Bookings confirm with an audit trail. Commissions calculate and settle automatically. Vendors confirm or the system escalates. Ledger balances are always current. Every team sees exactly what they need — nothing more.
