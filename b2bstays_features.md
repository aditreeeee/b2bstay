# B2BStays Hotel Portal — Features Overview

A complete B2B hotel management platform for hotels to manage travel agents, bookings, finances, transport, marketing, and vendor operations — all in one place.

---

## 1. Hotel Bookings

### Agent Bookings
View and manage all bookings made by B2B travel agents across channels. Filter by property, city, channel, commission status, check-in/out dates, or booking date. Export results to CSV.

### Group Quotation Requests
Handle group booking requests (10+ rooms) from B2B agents. Reply with a custom quote, negotiate pricing, and track status (Awaiting Reply / Confirmed / Rejected). Each request shows agent, event type, dates, room count, pax, and total value.

### Commission Payable
Track all commissions owed to B2B agents. Settle per booking (Select & Knock Off) or pay all pending dues for an agent in one go. Supports Bank Transfer (NEFT) with optional payment reference.

### Payments
Generate and track Razorpay payment links for group quotation advances and full payments. View total received, links awaiting payment, and manage individual payment records per quotation.

---

## 2. City Ledger

### Accounts & Ledger
Maintain credit accounts for corporates and travel agents. View real-time balance due, unassigned credits, total charges, and total payments. Filter by account name or period, and download statements.

### Manage Accounts
Create and manage corporate and travel agent accounts enrolled in the City Ledger. Edit contact details and toggle accounts on or off.

### Analytics
Visual breakdown of all-time charges, payments, and balance due per account — ranked by share of charges with progress bars. Instantly see which accounts carry the most outstanding balances.

### Charge Items & Banking
*(Accessible from the City Ledger section)* Log charge items and banking entries to keep ledger records accurate.

---

## 3. Hotel Packages

### Manage Packages
Create and publish multi-day stay packages with itinerary, gallery, and tiered pricing — made available to B2B agents for sale. Packages support tags, per-person or per-occupancy pricing, pax range, and night count.

### Package Bookings & Quote Requests
Receive and manage instant bookings and negotiated quote requests from agents against hotel packages. Track status (Confirmed / Needs Reply / Rejected) with full booking details per request.

### Package Commission
Track and settle commissions owed to agents on confirmed package bookings. Supports per-booking knock-off or a single bulk payment per agent.

---

## 4. Transportation

### Availability Calendar
Hour-by-hour visual calendar of the entire vehicle fleet. Shows confirmed bookings, holds, blocks, and turnaround buffers per vehicle. Filter by supplier, location, status, or category.

### Cab Bookings
Create and manage cab bookings made by sub-agents or hotel staff — airport pickups/drops, local hourly rentals, and multi-day trips. Track status across Pending Confirmation, Vehicle Assigned, Trip In Progress, and Completed.

### Vehicle Allocation
Assign specific vehicles to bookings that are awaiting a vehicle.

### Fleet Management
Maintain a master list of vehicles with registration numbers, categories (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, Tempo Traveller), and availability status.

### Manual Blocks
Block vehicles manually for maintenance, internal use, or any operational reason.

### Operating Cities
Define the cities where transport operations are active.

### Masters
Manage all transport reference data: vehicle categories, suppliers/fleet operators, locations, drivers, and rate cards.

### Company Profile & Settings
Configure transport company details and operational settings.

---

## 5. Marketing

### WhatsApp Messaging
Send messages to guests and leads directly from the hotel's own WhatsApp Business number. Includes an inbox view of all outbound conversations.

### Templates
Create and manage reusable message templates for WhatsApp and Email — for booking confirmations, reminders, promotions, and more.

### Campaigns
Send WhatsApp or Email campaigns in bulk to travel agents and corporates. Select template, define audience, and track delivery (Sent / Failed / Skipped).

### Marketing Calendar
Visual calendar to plan and schedule marketing activity across channels.

### Content Approval Queue
Review and approve content before it goes live.

### Prompt Library
Store and organize AI prompts for generating marketing content efficiently.

### Image Library
Centralized media library for storing and reusing images across campaigns and templates.

### Social Media
Connect and manage social media accounts for integrated marketing.

---

## 6. Reputation

### Reviews
Read and respond to guest reviews, bridged from the hotel's connected reviews account — all within the portal.

---

## 7. Agent Settings

### Agents & Channel Mapping
Map every B2B travel agent to a specific distribution channel for accurate channel-wise reporting. Set per-agent commission type (Net or Commission %) and rates. Bulk-map all global agents to a default channel at once.

### Channels Master List
View all active B2B channels (live and custom) configured for the property.

---

## 8. Vendors & Staff Alerts

### Vendor Master
Maintain a master list of external service vendors — hotels, transport operators, tour guides, and other suppliers used to fulfil bookings.

### Message Templates
Manage templates used for vendor communication (WhatsApp and Email).

### Package Vendors & Package Day Vendors
Assign vendors to specific packages and package days to enable automated approval workflows.

### Vendor Approval Requests
Automatically route booking service requests to vendors for confirmation. Track multi-attempt approval chains with escalation to backup vendors on timeout.

### Vendor Approved List
View all confirmed vendor assignments across active bookings.

### Staff Alerts
Real-time alert feed for events requiring staff attention — vendor unavailability, timeout escalations, expired documents (e.g., driver licences, fitness certificates), and more. Alerts are prioritised (High) and actionable (Approve / Mark as Read / Mark as Resolved).

### Alert Preferences
Configure which alerts each staff member receives and how.

### Vendor Reports & Approval History
Review historical vendor approval activity and generate reports.

---

## 9. Notifications

System-wide notification centre for all platform activity — bookings, payments, approvals, and alerts.

---

## 10. Settings

Property-level configuration covering integrations (Razorpay, WhatsApp Business, Reviews), channel setup, user roles, and platform preferences.
